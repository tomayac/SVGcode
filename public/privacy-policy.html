Privacy Policy

SVGcode (svgco.de and the Android app de.svgco.twa). Last updated: November 15, 2026.

SVGcode converts raster images such as JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP, and AVIF to vector graphics in SVG format. This Privacy Policy explains how the SVGcode website and the SVGcode Android app, which displays that website, handle your data.

Summary

SVGcode does not collect, store, or process any personal data on a server. It has no user accounts, no ads, and no analytics or tracking code.

Images you open

Images that you open, paste, drop, or share into SVGcode are converted entirely on your device, inside the browser. They are never uploaded, and the developer never sees them. The resulting SVG leaves your device only when you save, copy, or share it yourself.

Data stored on your device

SVGcode keeps your conversion settings, your language choice, and a reference to the last file you opened in your browser's local storage, so that they are still there on your next visit. This data stays on your device. You can remove it at any time by clearing the site data for svgco.de in your browser, or by clearing the app's storage in the Android settings.

Permissions

SVGcode asks for access to files or the clipboard only when you use a feature that needs it, such as opening or saving a file, or copying and pasting an image. That access is used for the action you started and for nothing else.

Hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives technical request data, such as your IP address, when your browser loads the site. The developer has no access to this data. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for details.

Data sharing

SVGcode does not sell or share any user data with third parties.

Children

SVGcode is a general-purpose tool. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes

Updates to this Privacy Policy are published on this page with a new date.
