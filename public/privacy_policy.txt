PRIVACY POLICY

SVGcode (https://svgco.de/ and the Android app de.svgco.twa)
Last updated: November 15, 2026

SVGcode does not collect, store, or process any personal data on a server. It
has no user accounts, no ads, and no analytics or tracking code. Images are
converted entirely on your device and are never uploaded. Settings stay in your
browser's local storage.

The full Privacy Policy is at https://svgco.de/privacy-policy.html
